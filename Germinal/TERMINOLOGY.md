# GERMINAL terminology

The commissioning unit is a **Reading**. A Reading may contain one primary form and evidence-supported alternate forms.

- Unpublished agent output: Reading proposal
- Passed commissioning record: Reading
- Opened learner view: Full Reading Card
- Twenty-five passed Readings: Pack
- Four hundred proposed Readings: Wave
- Failed or unsupported proposal: Quarantined Reading

Agents must use Reading/Readings in reports and status metadata.
