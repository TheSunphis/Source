# Germinal complete segmentation contract v9

Every displayed Japanese line must be decomposed at meaningful lexical, idiomatic, grammatical, auxiliary/inflectional, productive-slot, and punctuation boundaries. One segment wrapping an entire multi-component line is not analysis.

Each segment requires ordered identity, exact Japanese and reading spans, one established kind, contextual meaning, grammatical role, lemma except punctuation, inflection detail when applicable, proposed Vocabulary candidates, deferred canonical disposition, and evidence identity.

Deterministic anti-placeholder floors supplement rather than replace linguistic judgment: lines of six or more Japanese characters need at least two segments; lines of ten or more need at least three; and every six-line Card needs at least twelve segments overall plus at least one grammatical, inflectional, or punctuation segment. Arbitrary character splitting is prohibited and remains reviewable by Crow.
